Video description (paste under the upload)

Campus Pulse answers the only question that matters at lunch: where do I actually go? Tell it how many of you there are and what you care about — closest, quietest, no queue, or all three — and it names one place, with live free seats, queue wait and walking time. Tap through for the seat map, ghost seats and the rest of campus. Ask it anything in plain language, including why it chose that place. The camera counts seats and throws the frame away; no video ever reaches the browser. Just the answer.

Built with Python, OpenCV and a pre-trained YOLO11 model. Live demo: localhost:3000

How to film it

  • Keep it under 60 seconds. The landing wordmark needs a beat to assemble — do not rush it.
  • Record at 1440 px wide or more. The type is the product, so let it fill the frame.
  • Show the chip count changing: one chip, then three chips, then the answer. That is the feature.
  • Open the arrow and scroll once, slowly. The list is the proof of depth.
  • End on the Ask AI answer, then tap “Ask why”. That is the moment judges remember.

Honest note for the description

Live camera accuracy was measured on a public eatery camera: the model draws real boxes but undercounts a wide, dim, crowded room (about 19% recall with yolo11n at 640, about 58% with yolo11s at 960). At table distance — the setup this demo uses — people are large in frame. Full method and numbers: docs/LIVE_FEED_CHECK.md.
